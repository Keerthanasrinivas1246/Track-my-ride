Cutting-Edge Features

Built For Daily Commuters & Achievers

Track My Ride bridges calendar productivity with real-world geographic coordinates so you never drive past an errand again.

Location-Aware Tasks

Link every task to a precise venue or address. Search places easily with Google Places Autocomplete or click directly on the interactive map.

Adjustable Geofence Radius

Configure custom alert distances (from 50m up to 5,000m). View radius circles live on map as you adjust your geofence sensitivity.

Multi-Channel Proximity Alerts

Enjoy desktop browser push notifications, in-app toast overlays, and pleasant synthesized audio chimes the instant you reach the target radius.

Realtime Nearby Tasks

Browser Geolocation calculates Haversine distances dynamically, ranking tasks closest to you along with estimated walking and driving times.

Interactive Calendar & Map

Switch effortlessly between full-screen Google Maps task views, monthly calendar matrices, or searchable grid and table dashboards.

Secure & Isolated Data

Built on Django's battle-tested authentication. Every user's tasks, notes, locations, and geofences remain strictly private and encrypted.

How Track My Ride Works

A seamless 3-step loop to never miss a location-critical task.

1
Pin Your Task

Create a task like "Buy Groceries at DMart". Search the venue or drop a marker to lock the coordinates and geofence radius.

2
Commute Freely

Go about your workday, commute, or travel. The system operates in the background monitoring GPS distance via the Geolocation API.

3
Get Prompted

Once within 500m (or your custom radius), hear the chime, receive the desktop notification, and tick the task off immediately!
